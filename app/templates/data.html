Benchmarks, and why two of them disagree

There is no single thing called “the index”. The file gives you several, and comparing them is the point.

SeriesWhat it actually is
^FTSEFTSE 100 price index. No dividends. The longest history.
CUKX.LiShares Core FTSE 100 accumulating ETF. Dividends reinvested inside the fund, so this genuinely is total return. Regress against this one.
FTAL.LSPDR FTSE UK All-Share accumulating ETF. Total return, wider universe.
